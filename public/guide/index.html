Choose your role

Rules that apply to everyone

AI is advisory

Anything with a purple AI badge is a suggestion. A person signs off, and the sign-off is logged.

Every action is recorded

Scores, overrides, sign-offs, chat messages, document opens, and denials go to the DELTA Ledger, a hash-chained log. The header shows Chain Verified when it is intact.

Reasons are required

Overrides need a written reason of at least 20 characters. Flags need a reason too.

No anchoring

Panelists and the Board President score first. The AI fit value, model score, and panel average appear only after they submit.

Panel chat is on the record

Every message, pin, and closed thread goes to the DELTA Ledger. No health information: the composer blocks it.

Talent data is walled off

Risk of Loss, Talent Score, and the N-Box never reach Panelists and never feed scoring or the Order of Merit.

Fictional data only

Every name, ID, and PAS code here is invented. Do not enter real personnel data. Reset Demo in the footer restores the start state.
